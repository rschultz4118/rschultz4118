# Job Posting Skills Comparison

## Posting 1: Business Program Manager, Compliance & Ethics
**Seniority:** Mid-to-senior level / Program management

**Required skills and responsibilities identified:**
- Translating regulatory requirements into controls and evidence
- Audit readiness, control testing, and remediation
- Compliance tracking
- Responsible AI safeguards and human oversight
- Program management and cross-functional collaboration
- Executive reporting and communication
- Process improvement and automation

**Additional qualifications identified:**
- Coaching and onboarding program managers
- Formal education or equivalent professional experience

## Posting 2: Manager, Fraud Policy and Strategy
**Seniority:** Management-level / Fraud strategy

**Required skills and responsibilities identified:**
- Fraud policy development and prevention
- Interpreting fraud intelligence and emerging threats
- Balancing fraud prevention with customer experience
- Influencing product and fraud prevention roadmaps
- Fraud prevention technologies and trust and safety operations
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Governance and strategic communication

**Preferred qualifications identified:**
- Fraud-specific industry experience

## Skills Shared Between Both Postings
- Program management
- Cross-functional collaboration
- Governance frameworks
- Executive reporting and communication
- Influencing without direct authority
- Automation and process improvement

## Reflection

What surprised me most was how much both positions emphasized business judgment and communication alongside technical skills. Although one position focuses on regulatory compliance and the other on fraud prevention, both require translating complex information into actionable recommendations and influencing decisions across departments. My existing compliance experience and Certified Fraud Examiner background provide a strong foundation for these responsibilities. Studying data science will help me strengthen the analytical skills needed to support the work I already do.
