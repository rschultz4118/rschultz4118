About Me

I'm a Certified Fraud Examiner (CFE) with over eight years of experience in financial services, regulatory compliance, fraud investigations, and program management. My professional background includes compliance monitoring and testing, internal audit, risk assessments, and evaluating business processes to identify weaknesses and opportunities for improvement.

Throughout my career, I've worked with regulatory requirements, large datasets, and complex business processes. My interest in data science developed as I began recognizing opportunities to use programming and automation to improve work that traditionally required extensive manual review. I've found that combining analytical tools with industry knowledge can make risk identification more efficient and help organizations make better decisions.

I'm currently pursuing a degree in Data Science at Oregon State University, where I'm strengthening my skills in Python, SQL, mathematics, data analysis, and software development. I'm particularly interested in applying these skills to financial risk management, fraud detection, and regulatory compliance.

My professional experience has also taught me the importance of communication, collaboration, and understanding the business implications of analytical findings. I want to continue building on that foundation by developing technical solutions that are practical, reliable, and useful to the people making decisions.

Ultimately, my goal is to bridge the gap between data analytics and business operations, helping financial institutions use information more effectively.
